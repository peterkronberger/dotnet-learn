# Audit M18, M19 and W1–W4 (issue #698, 2026-11-25)

Scope: the plan sections `### M18`, `### M19`, `### W1`, `### W2`, `### W3` and `### W4` in `concept/module-plan.md` against the lessons, examples and quiz files of these modules.

Method: lesson list and headings, then keyword searches (German and English) in the lessons, the example folders and the quiz files. For the review modules W1–W4 the checks were: (1) two lessons with the planned titles, (2) every concept of the section is asked in at least one task or quiz question, (3) every misconception id of the section appears as `"misconception"` (single choice) or in `distractorMisconceptions` (Parsons) in `website/src/data/quiz/de/w0N.json`.

No lesson, example or quiz file was changed. The three gaps below are small in content, but all three review lessons already sit at the upper time limit (for example `w01-l01` has 28 quiz tasks and `w01-l02` about 700 words), so a fix would push them over 25 minutes. They are listed as `large-gap` for the final issue to decide (a new task in a lesson that gets another task removed, or a plan correction that drops the concept).
